# Sprint Planning Agenda

**Purpose:** Plan the work to be performed during the Sprint.

**Timebox:** Maximum 8 hours for a 1-month Sprint (shorter for shorter Sprints)

---

## Topic 1: Why Is This Sprint Valuable?

- **Input:** Product Backlog, Product Goal
- **Outcome:** Sprint Goal

### Discussion Points
- What is the most valuable outcome we can deliver this Sprint?
- How does this align with the Product Goal?
- What are the stakeholders' priorities?

**Accountability:** The Product Owner proposes how the product could increase its value and utility in the Sprint. The entire Scrum Team collaborates to define the Sprint Goal.

---

## Topic 2: What Can Be Done This Sprint?

- **Input:** Product Backlog, latest Increment, projected capacity, past performance
- **Outcome:** Selected Product Backlog Items

### Discussion Points
- What can we realistically deliver this Sprint?
- What is our capacity?
- What did we learn from past performance?

**Accountability:** The Developers select the items they forecast to complete. The Product Owner helps clarify the value and priorities.

---

## Topic 3: How Will the Work Get Done?

- **Input:** Selected Product Backlog Items, Sprint Goal
- **Outcome:** Sprint Backlog (plan by and for Developers)

### Discussion Points
- How will we turn the selected items into a Done Increment?
- What are the technical tasks required?
- How will we collaborate?

**Accountability:** The Developers plan the work necessary to create the Increment. **No one else tells them how to do it.**
