## Review Guidance

Update this dashboard:

- **Daily** — blockers, status changes, metrics
- **Weekly** — milestones, risks, accomplishments
- **Per Sprint** — velocity, burndown, resource health
- **Before stakeholder meetings** — ensure data is current

## Notes

- Keep this dashboard **single-page** and **scannable**.
- Use **traffic-light indicators** (🟢🟡🔴) for quick status.
- Link to detailed artifacts rather than duplicating them.
- **The dashboard is not the source of truth — it's the window into it.**
- Customize metrics based on your delivery approach (Agile, hybrid, or predictive).
