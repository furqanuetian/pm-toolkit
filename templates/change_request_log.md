## Decision Guide

| Decision | Definition |
|----------|------------|
| **Approved** | Change is accepted and will be implemented |
| **Rejected** | Change is not accepted; rationale documented |
| **Deferred** | Change is postponed for future consideration |
| **Pending** | Change is under review |

---

## Review Guidance

Review and update this log:

- During Sprint Reviews
- When new change requests are submitted
- When change requests are approved or rejected
- During backlog refinement sessions

---

## Notes

- Assess the impact on scope, schedule, budget, quality, and risk before approving implementation.
- Keep rejected requests to maintain an audit trail.
- Update the Decision Log if a formal governance decision is made.
- Update the Requirements Traceability Matrix if approved changes affect requirements.
- Communicate approved changes according to the Communication Plan.
- Close the change request only after implementation has been verified.
