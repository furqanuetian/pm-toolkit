## Review Guidance

Capture lessons:

- During Sprint Retrospectives
- After major milestones
- When significant issues are resolved
- During project closure

Review and update this register:

- During project closure
- Before initiating similar projects
- When implementing process improvements

---

## Notes

- Capture observations as soon as they are identified.
- Focus on actionable recommendations, not just observations.
- Avoid assigning blame; focus on improving processes, communication, and delivery practices.
- Link lessons to RAID Log items, Decision Log entries, or Change Requests where applicable.
- Review lessons before starting new projects to avoid repeating mistakes.
