# Project Kickoff Checklist

**Purpose:** Ensure every project starts with clear alignment, shared understanding, and a path to delivery.

---

## 🔍 Pre-Kickoff (Before the Meeting)

- [ ] Review project charter or initial request
- [ ] Identify key stakeholders and their roles
- [ ] Schedule kickoff meeting (60-90 minutes)
- [ ] Prepare draft agenda and share in advance
- [ ] Gather existing documentation (e.g., BRD, PRD, user stories)
- [ ] Confirm availability of key decision-makers

---

## 📋 Kickoff Meeting Agenda

### 1. Introductions & Context (5 min)
- Who is in the room?
- Why are we here?
- What is the product or outcome?

### 2. Project Overview & Objectives (10 min)
- What are we building?
- Why is it important?
- How does it align with business goals?

### 3. Scope: In-Scope vs. Out-of-Scope (15 min)
- What is included in this project?
- What is explicitly not included?
- What are the assumptions?

### 4. Key Stakeholders & Communication (10 min)
- Who are the key stakeholders?
- How will we communicate?
- How often will we sync?

### 5. Timeline & Milestones (15 min)
- Key milestones and dates
- Dependencies and constraints
- What happens if we miss a milestone?

### 6. Risks, Assumptions, & Dependencies (10 min)
- What could go wrong?
- What are we assuming?
- Who depends on us, and who do we depend on?

### 7. Next Steps & Action Items (5 min)
- Who owns what?
- What happens next?
- When is the next sync?

---

## ✅ Post-Kickoff

- [ ] Send meeting summary with action items
- [ ] Create shared project folder (e.g., Notion, SharePoint, Confluence)
- [ ] Set up recurring checkpoint meetings (e.g., weekly syncs)
- [ ] Publish project charter and RAID log
- [ ] Share access to project tools (JIRA, Asana, etc.)

---

## 📌 Key Questions to Answer Before the First Sprint

- What is the Product Goal?
- Who is the Product Owner?
- Who are the Developers?
- What is the Definition of Done?
- What is the initial Product Backlog?
